# Principia Mathematica ✱1–✱5 and ✱9–✱10 in Lean 4

A deep embedding of the propositional part of Whitehead & Russell's *Principia Mathematica* (1910), and of its theory of one apparent variable (✱9–✱10).

- `PM/Basic.lean`: formulas built from variables, `∼` and `∨`. The definitions ✱1·01, ✱3·01 and ✱4·01 are macros. `Thm` is exactly PM's primitive propositions ✱1·1–✱1·6. The file also proves soundness and gives a counter-model test.
- `PM/Statements.lean`: the statement of every ✱2–✱5 proposition, as read from the 1910 printed scan, and each checked to be a tautology by `decide`. It was generated by `data/gen_lean.py` from the companion text audit's proposition list and parser, which are not in this repository; the checked-in `PM/Statements.lean` is the artifact the proofs use, so building needs nothing else. (The script also writes to an older path, `lean/PM/Statements.lean`; adjust before rerunning.)
- `PM/Replay.lean`: 103 propositions replayed as explicit `Thm` terms built from PM's own cited propositions. 72 use only the propositions PM cites; 31 also need at least one proposition PM does not cite, and each addition is recorded in `data/replay_ledger.json`.
- `PM/Derived.lean` and `PM/Replay2.lean`: derived rules (syllogism, ∧/≡ introduction and elimination, a deduction theorem) and the remaining 85 propositions.
- `PM/Complete.lean`: a Kalmár-style completeness theorem, `complete : p.isTaut = true → Thm p`. No replay depends on it.
- `PM/Star2.lean`: shows that Gutenberg #78050's transcription of ✱1·4 is not a theorem; the 1910 print has `q ∨ p`.

Status: all 188 ✱2–✱5 propositions are proved. Every PM theorem (✱2–✱5, ✱9–✱10) and `complete` reports "does not depend on any axioms" under `#print axioms`. The project contains no `sorry`, no `native_decide` and no added axioms. An exhaustive check of all 939 declarations under `PM` (`AllAxioms.lean`) finds 13 that use Lean's standard axioms `propext` and `Quot.sound` (never `Classical.choice`): compiler-generated `injEq` lemmas and a `Repr` instance, and three metatheorems about the embedding, namely the soundness theorem `PM.sound`, `PM.not_thm_of_countermodel` and `PM.gutenberg_1_4_refuted`. None of them is a PM derivation.

Fidelity in one line (✱2–✱5, from the two ledgers): 112 proofs use only the propositions PM cites (72 in `Replay.lean`, 40 in `Replay2.lean`); 65 follow PM's proof but need at least one further proposition, each listed (31 + 34); 11 are not yet rewritten toward PM's lines.

`data/replay_ledger.json` and `data/replay2_ledger.json` record, for each proposition, which of PM's cited propositions the Lean proof uses.

In `PM/Replay2.lean`, 74 of the 85 proofs follow PM's own proof. Each has one Lean line per Dem. line, or, where PM prints only a bracketed hint, one per hinted proposition in PM's order. The only steps done silently are the ones PM itself leaves tacit; the file header lists them. The ledger's `fidelity` field is `pm-lines` for 40 proofs. It is `pm-lines+extra` for 34 proofs, which also need a proposition PM does not cite; `extra_used` and `reason` say which one and why. It is `unchanged` for 11 proofs whose transcription has no Dem. or hint to follow.

## ✱9–✱10: apparent variables

This part follows ✱10's "alternative method" (PM pp. 143–144): `∼`, `∨` and `(x)` are primitive for formulas with or without apparent variables, and ✱1's primitive propositions hold for all of them. Only individual variables are quantified; PM's ramified hierarchy of types and orders (✱12 onward) is **not** formalized yet. The design note `docs/STAR9_DESIGN.md` compares this with ✱9's own method and a higher-order embedding.

- `PM/Quant.lean`: formulas `QF V` over a type `V` of individual variables. The function φx̂ is a Lean function `V → QF V`, so there is no substitution and no variable capture. ✱10·01–✱10·03 are macros. `QThm` is ✱1·1–✱1·6 for all formulas, plus ✱10·1, ✱10·11 (a rule) and ✱10·12. The file proves soundness and gives three counter-models. `liftThm` carries any ✱1–✱5 theorem over to `QF`.
- `PM/QuantLift.lean`: all 188 ✱2–✱5 propositions restated for `QF` (generated by `data/gen_quant_lift.py`).
- `PM/Star10.lean`: ✱9·1, ✱9·11, ✱9·12, ✱9·13, ✱9·2, ✱9·21–✱9·25, and ✱10·1–✱10·57 (50 propositions; ✱10·121, ✱10·122 and ✱10·221 concern significance and are built into the syntax). The proofs follow PM's lines; each docstring names the steps PM leaves tacit.

`AxiomCheck.lean` (generated by `data/gen_axiomcheck.py`) prints the axioms of a selection of 460 declarations (the 188 ✱2–✱5 theorems, `complete`, and the theorems and models of the ✱9–✱10 files); all report "does not depend on any axioms". `AllAxioms.lean` checks every declaration under `PM` (see Status). A clean `lake build` takes about 15 s.

**Scope of the ✱9–✱10 semantics.** `QF V` uses functions `V → QF V` for φx̂ (higher-order abstract syntax). Lean allows such functions to inspect their argument, so `QF V` also contains "exotic" terms that correspond to no finite formula of PM. The soundness theorem `qsound` holds for all of them in every model, so nothing false becomes provable; but there is no adequacy theorem (a translation showing that `QF`/`QThm` correspond exactly to PM's finite formulas and derivations). The ✱9–✱10 theorems are stated for arbitrary φ, so they also hold for these terms.

Companion audit of the digital texts against the printed page: [principia-mathematica-text-audit](https://github.com/willmcginnis/principia-mathematica-text-audit) (public tracker).

## Building and checking

Requires the Lean toolchain named in `lean-toolchain` (via [elan](https://github.com/leanprover/elan)); no other dependencies.

```
lake build                      # builds every file
lake env lean AxiomCheck.lean   # the 460 selected declarations; all report "does not depend on any axioms"
lake env lean AllAxioms.lean    # every declaration under PM; lists the few that use propext / Quot.sound
```

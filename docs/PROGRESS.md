# Progress

How much of *Principia Mathematica* is formalized, by volume, range and chapter, and the milestones. Every
table between the markers below is generated by `scripts/progress.py` ([scripts/README.md](../scripts/README.md))
from this repository's own files; do not edit them by hand. The script reads the Lean files and ledgers at a
git commit (default `HEAD`) and the item lists in `data/`.

## Units

Different files count different things. These are the units, and how they relate.

- **Numbered assertion.** A numbered item (✱N·M) that PM prints with the assertion sign ⊢ and that is not a
  definition (Df), a primitive proposition (Pp) or a rule stated in words. This is the unit of the tables
  below and of the [formalization comparison](FORMALIZATION_COMPARISON.md). Vol I has 3,230 of them among
  3,449 numbered items (`data/vol1_items.json`).
  - **Stated**: a theorem is named for its PM number (`t<c>_<d>` for ✱2–✱5, `q<c>_<d>` from ✱9 on).
  - **Proved**: stated, in a file with no `sorry`, `axiom` or `native_decide`, and not merely a primitive
    rule restated.
  - **Followed PM's lines**: proved, with a ledger row saying the proof follows PM's (`pm-lines` or
    `pm-lines+extra`; in `replay_ledger.json`, `FAITHFUL` or `REPAIRED`). See [FIDELITY.md](FIDELITY.md).
- **Primitive-rule restatement.** A numbered assertion of PM that is a primitive rule of the embedding, so
  its "proof" is the rule itself. There are four: ✱9·2, ✱9·25, ✱10·1 and ✱10·12. They count as stated, not
  as proved. PM's own primitive propositions (✱9·1–✱9·13, ✱12·1, ✱12·11) and rules stated in words (such as
  ✱10·11) are not assertions at all, though some have theorems named for them.
- **Helper or variant.** A declaration that is not one PM numbered item: for example `q12_1_var`,
  `q12_1_of_var`, `q14_21_neg`, `q10_11_27`. Not counted.
- **Lifted copy.** `PM.QL.t…` in `PM/QuantLift.lean`: each ✱2–✱5 theorem restated for formulas with apparent
  variables. A copy, so not counted again.

The checks use a wider unit. `RedCheck.lean` and `StyleCheck.lean` treat **481 declarations as "PM
propositions"**: 188 lifted copies, 272 numbered assertions from ✱9 on (268 proved and the 4 primitive-rule
restatements), 6 primitive propositions, 6 rules stated in words, and 9 helpers or variants. The 456 proved
assertions of the tables are those 268 plus the 188 ✱2–✱5 theorems of `PM/Replay.lean` and
`PM/Replay2.lean`. (These figures are for commit `e73ab2b`.)

The proofs check when the project builds; which Lean axioms they use is in [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md).

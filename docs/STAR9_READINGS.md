## Where the exchange rule is used

What is counted (Checks/Star9Readings.lean). The rule is ✱8·13 (`Thm9.exch`) in C and Switch
(`Thm9.switch`) in L. For a Lean declaration, *direct* is the number of occurrences of the rule in
its own proof term, and *through lemmas* the number of occurrences in the proof terms of the
declaration and of every declaration it uses, directly or through others, each counted once. A
lemma stated for other readings only is not followed (a lemma stated for every reading reaches it
only in another reading's branch), so *through lemmas* is an upper bound on what the reading's
proof uses. Each occurrence of ✱8·13 is also listed by the kinds of the two quantifiers it
exchanges, (x)(∃y) or (∃x)(∃y) (none of (∃x)(y) or (x)(y) occurs). For a combination of p. 142 the
search is run again on the reading's proposition, first without the rule, then with it; *steps*
is the number of steps of the rule in the proof found (each use of a lemma counts that lemma's
occurrences). "Derived under A" means derived by our proofs under A's definitions, without a
rule, as the same proposition; "not derived" means not derived by our proofs, and whether ✱9's
rules derive it by a longer route stays open.
